## Request Body

<ParamField body="memberId" type="string">
  The unique identifier of the member to whom the credit will be added.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customerId" type="string">
  The unique identifier of the customer to whom the credit will be added.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="productId" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the product associated with the credit.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="membershipTierId" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the membership tier associated with the credit.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="integer" required>
  The amount of credit to add (positive integer).
</ParamField>

<Note>
  At least **one of** `memberId` **or** `customerId` must be provided.
</Note>

## Response

Successful Response

### Main Structure (Root)

<ResponseField name="statusCode" type="integer">
  Status code from API.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="message" type="string">
  Status message that describes the status code (`"success"` on success).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  Object containing details of the added credit and the resulting balance.
</ResponseField>

### data Structure (Object)

<ResponseField name="customerId" type="string">
  The unique identifier of the customer to whom the credit was added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="productId" type="string">
  The unique identifier of the product associated with the credit added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="membershipTierId" type="string">
  The unique identifier of the membership tier associated with the credit added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="amount" type="number">
  The amount of credit that was added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="customerBalance" type="number">
  The credit balance after adding the credit.
</ResponseField>

### Errors

<ResponseField name="400 Validation Error" type="object">
  Returned when required fields are missing or invalid. Validation errors use the
  PLURAL `messages` key with a `data` array of field errors:
  `{ "statusCode": 400, "messages": "Validation Error", "data": [ ... ] }`.
</ResponseField>
